EXPERIMENTAL PHYSICS · RADIO DETECTION · SIGNAL PROCESSING

Turning weak, distributed signals into reliable physical measurements.

I build precision sensing and reconstruction systems at the intersection of detector physics, RF signal processing and scientific computing.

My doctoral work in the Pierre Auger Collaboration focuses on radio interferometry for very inclined cosmic-ray air showers: synchronising detector stations, validating timing, modelling systematics and reconstructing coherent signals across a large array. The same measurement and inference methods transfer naturally to radar, remote sensing and Earth-observation systems.
