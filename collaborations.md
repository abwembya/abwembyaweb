<h1>Collaborations</h1>
<p class="page-intro">My work has developed through international collaborations spanning cosmic-ray observatories, collider instrumentation, distributed detector systems, nuclear measurement laboratories, and underground science.</p>

<section class="section">
  <h2 class="section-title"><span class="section-icon" aria-hidden="true"><i class="fas fa-users"></i></span>International Research Collaborations</h2>
  <div class="card-grid">
    <article class="content-card">
      <p class="eyebrow">2021 – 2026 · Argentina and the Netherlands</p>
      <h3 class="collaboration-title"><a href="https://www.auger.org/collaboration/" rel="noopener">Pierre Auger Collaboration</a></h3>
      <p>Doctoral research with the Pierre Auger Observatory through Radboud University and the Nikhef research programme, focused on the AugerPrime Radio Detector and the Auger Engineering Radio Array.</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Precision timing, phase calibration and detector synchronisation</li>
        <li>Radio-interferometric reconstruction of inclined air showers</li>
        <li>Antenna-response, geometry, noise and atmospheric-systematics studies</li>
        <li>Long-distance DAQ chains, remote monitoring and data-quality control</li>
        <li>Fluorescence-camera and LiDAR calibration and operations</li>
      </ul>
      <p><a href="https://www.auger.org/" rel="noopener">Pierre Auger Observatory</a> · <a href="https://www.nikhef.nl/en/programs/auger/" rel="noopener">Auger at Nikhef</a></p>
    </article>

    <article class="content-card">
      <p class="eyebrow">2020 – 2021 · Japan and Europe</p>
      <h3 class="collaboration-title"><a href="https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/822070" rel="noopener">JENNIFER2</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nima.2019.03.071" rel="noopener">LUMIBELLE2</a> · <a href="https://www.belle2.org/" rel="noopener">Belle II</a></h3>
      <p>Worked with the LUMIBELLE2 group through the JENNIFER2 programme on fast luminosity measurements for the Belle II experiment at KEK's SuperKEKB electron-positron collider.</p>
      <ul>
        <li>CVD diamond-sensor channels and the Zero-Degree Luminosity Monitor</li>
        <li>ROOT and EPICS luminosity-data analysis</li>
        <li>Comparison with ECL luminosity measurements</li>
        <li>DAQ monitoring, archiving and troubleshooting</li>
      </ul>
      <p>The work was supervised by Philip Bambade of IJCLab, CNRS/IN2P3, Université Paris-Saclay, with whom I have remained in professional contact.</p>
      <p><a href="https://www.belle2.org/" rel="noopener">Belle II</a> · <a href="https://www.kek.jp/en/" rel="noopener">KEK</a></p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
