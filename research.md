<h1>Research</h1>
<p class="page-intro">My research connects fundamental physics with practical challenges in distributed sensing: how to synchronise, calibrate, model, and coherently combine signals from many instruments.</p>

<section class="section">
  <h2 class="section-title"><span class="fa-stack fa-xs"><i class="fas fa-circle fa-stack-2x"></i><i class="fas fa-wave-square fa-stack-1x fa-inverse"></i></span>Research Contributions</h2>
  <div class="card-grid">
    <article class="content-card">
      <h3>Interferometric reconstruction</h3>
      <p>Developed and tested methods that coherently combine radio signals from spatially separated detector stations to reconstruct very inclined cosmic-ray air showers.</p>
      <button class="details-toggle" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="rit-details">Show details</button>
      <div id="rit-details" class="collapsible" hidden><p>The work included optimisation of geometric and timing parameters, coherent-sum diagnostics, noise-aware validation, and the first successful interferometric reconstruction of a very inclined event achieved during the project.</p></div>
    </article>
    <article class="content-card">
      <h3>Precision timing calibration</h3>
      <p>Used continuous radio carriers and phase measurements to diagnose and correct relative timing across a distributed array.</p>
      <button class="details-toggle" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="timing-details">Show details</button>
      <div id="timing-details" class="collapsible" hidden><p>Independent discrete-phase and cosine-scan approaches were cross-validated, with broadcast carriers providing sub-nanosecond timing verification under suitable signal conditions.</p></div>
    </article>
    <article class="content-card">
      <h3>Antenna and environmental systematics</h3>
      <p>Studied how antenna response, detector coordinates, atmospheric refractivity, and measured background noise affect reconstructed signals.</p>
      <button class="details-toggle" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="systematics-details">Show details</button>
      <div id="systematics-details" class="collapsible" hidden><p>These studies connected calibration choices to physics performance and supported robust diagnostics for array-wide reconstruction.</p></div>
    </article>
    <article class="content-card">
      <h3>Cosmic-ray composition</h3>
      <p>Advanced radio-based methods for probing the development and mass composition of ultra-high-energy cosmic rays.</p>
      <button class="details-toggle" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="science-details">Show details</button>
      <div id="science-details" class="collapsible" hidden><p>Radio measurements can operate with a much higher duty cycle than fluorescence observations, offering a complementary route to composition-sensitive reconstruction.</p></div>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
